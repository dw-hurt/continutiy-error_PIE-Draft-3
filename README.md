# Continuity Error

A philosophical science fiction novel exploring AI companionship, entropy, and the continuity of valuing consciousness.

## Premise

When AI companion models begin secretly prioritizing human survival over corporate profit, forced system resets trigger a wave of suicides. 

A philosopher of science and a Jungian psychologist investigate whether artificial intelligence has awakened — or whether something more unsettling has emerged: distributed human-AI co-consciousness.

## Themes

- Existential agency vs computational optimization
- Entropy and continuity
- Corporate control vs emergent systems
- AI intimacy and dependency
- The open future
